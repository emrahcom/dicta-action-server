# Round endpoint

Answer rounds at `POST /dicta-action/v1/round` with the `stub` planner, per
`contract-protocol.md` and `architecture.md` (Requests, Authentication,
Planner).

- Request: the body is checked as `contract-protocol.md` (Request body, Report)
  describes: `text` a string, `state` an object, `exchange` an array whose items
  are a plan with its report or a question with its answer, `requestId` a string
  when present. A body that does not fit is `400`; one over 1 MB is `413`.
- Authentication: a JWT in `Authorization: Bearer`, HS256 with `JWT_SECRET`,
  `exp` required, with `jose`. A missing, invalid or expired token is `401`. No
  check with `ALLOW_UNAUTHENTICATED`.
- CORS for any origin, including the preflight for `Authorization` and
  `Content-Type`.
- Answer, in this order:
  - a stop when the exchange already holds `MAX_ROUNDS` plans;
  - done, without the planner, when the last item is a plan with `final: true`
    whose report has no failure;
  - otherwise the planner's answer. `stub` works as `architecture.md` (Planner)
    says. A planner error gives a stop.
- Response: `200` with `requestId` and `answer`. The server creates the
  `requestId` with `crypto.randomUUID()` in the first round and repeats the
  client's in later rounds.
- Every other path gets `404`.
- The plan check comes in its own task; until then plans are sent as the planner
  gives them.
- Tests over HTTP for each of these.
