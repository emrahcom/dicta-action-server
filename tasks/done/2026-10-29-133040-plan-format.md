# Plan format

Bring the plan check up to `contract-plan.md`.

- A `forEach` has `forEach` and `do`, and no `as`. Inside its `do`, `$item` is
  the current item, with a path as usual (References).
- `args` has no properties its capability's schema does not name, at every level
  where the schema lists `properties` (Failures).
- A top-level step may have `includeOutput`, a boolean. A call in a `do` may not
  (Include output, Failures).
- A plan is at most 16 KB, counted as the UTF-8 bytes of its JSON (Limits).
- The check that a capability that loads a page is the last step goes away; any
  step may change the page (`contract-capability.md`, Page changes).
- Tests for each.
