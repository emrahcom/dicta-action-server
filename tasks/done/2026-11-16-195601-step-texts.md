# Step texts

A top-level step can have a `text`, shown while that step runs
(`contract-plan.md`, Label, Steps, Failures and Limits;
`architecture-planner.md`, Rules for the LLM).

Plan check (`src/plan.ts`): a top-level `call` or `forEach` may have `text`, a
string of at most 100 characters. A call in a `do` with `text` fails the check,
as with `includeOutput`.

Reply (`src/round.ts`, `checkReply`): each top-level step's `text` is cut to 100
characters before the check, like the label, with the step in the log, such as
`steps[2].text cut`.

Claude planner (`src/planners/claude.ts`):

- The reply schema: `text`, a string, on a step; not on a call in `do`.
- The plan format: a top-level step may have "text": one short sentence the user
  sees while that step runs, at most 100 characters.
- Rules: give a step a "text" only when it takes a while, such as a long loop or
  a slow call; add a step's text to the texts in the language of the request.

Tests: a top-level call and a `forEach` with a text pass; a text of 100
characters passes and of 101 fails the check; a call in `do` with a text fails;
a long step text from the planner is cut; the schema has `text` on a step but
not in `do`.

Check: `npm run check`, `npm run lint` and `npm run test` pass.
