# Request rules

Bring the round's checks and rules up to `contract-protocol.md` and
`architecture.md`.

- `MAX_ROUNDS` becomes `MAX_PLANS`, a whole number from 1 to 20, default 10
  (`architecture.md`, Configuration). The cancel and final rules come first. The
  limit applies only when the planner answers with a new plan: then the round
  gets a stop instead. Every other answer goes through (`architecture.md`,
  Requests).
- `time` with `Z` is refused; the offset is written as numbers
  (`contract-protocol.md`, Request body).
- A report has `outputs`, an object from step numbers to outputs, and
  `tooLarge`, an object from step numbers to sizes, both optional. `failure` has
  a `code` and a `message`, and may have `step`, `item`, `do` and `outputs`
  (`contract-protocol.md`, Report).
- A part over its limit gets a `400` that names it (`contract-protocol.md`,
  Limits and Errors): `text`, each answer and each added text over 10000
  characters (Unicode code points); `state` over 16 KB; a failure `message` over
  200 characters; the included outputs of a report, `outputs` and
  `failure.outputs` together, over 16 KB. Bytes are the UTF-8 bytes of the JSON.
- Tests for each.
