# Optional args

A capability may leave out `args` when it takes no arguments; a plan step that
calls it has `"args": {}` (`contract-capability.md`, Capabilities;
`contract-context.md`, Fields; `contract-plan.md`, Failures).

- The context check accepts a capability without `args` and checks `args` like
  before when it is set.
- The plan check accepts only `{}` as the `args` of a step that calls a
  capability without `args`, and refuses other arguments.
- Tests for each.
