# Halt

The report's `failure` is now `halt`, with the same fields: `step`, `item`,
`do`, `code`, `message` and `outputs` (`contract-protocol.md`, Report).

- The server reads `halt` in the report: its checks, its limits and its error
  texts, and the cancel and final rules (`architecture.md`, Planner).
- A report with `failure` is no longer read as one with a halt.
- Tests for each.
