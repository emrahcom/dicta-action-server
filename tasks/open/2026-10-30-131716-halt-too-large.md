# Halt too large

A report's `halt` may have `tooLarge`: the size in bytes of `halt.outputs` when
it did not fit and was left out (`contract-protocol.md`, Report).

- The report check accepts `halt.tooLarge` as an optional whole number, like
  `step`, `item` and `do`, and refuses any other value with a `400`.
- Tests for each.
