# Answer check

Check every answer of the planner before it is sent, not only plans
(`contract-protocol.md`, Response body; `architecture.md`, Planner).

- `type` is `plan`, `question`, `done` or `stop`.
- A question and a stop have a `text`, a string. A done may have one.
- A question's `options`, when present, is a list of strings.
- A `text` is at most 200 characters (Unicode code points). There are at most 5
  `options`, each at most 50 characters (`contract-protocol.md`, Limits).
- An answer that fails the check is never sent: the round gets a stop instead,
  and the reason is logged as a warning, as for a plan.
- Tests for each.
