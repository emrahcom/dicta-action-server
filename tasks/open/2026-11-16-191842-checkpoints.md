# Checkpoints

Every `PLANS_PER_CHECKPOINT` plans, the user is asked whether to go on, and
`MAX_PLANS` becomes the site owner's higher limit (`architecture.md`, Requests
and Configuration; `contract-plan.md`, Checkpoint).

Configuration (`src/index.ts`):

- `PLANS_PER_CHECKPOINT`: a whole number from 1 to 20, default 10; any other
  value fails at startup, as `MAX_PLANS` does.
- `MAX_PLANS`: from 1 to 100, default 50.

Plan (`src/plan.ts`):

- Two new optional fields: `checkpoint`, a boolean, and `progress`, a string of
  at most 200 characters. The plan check accepts both, so a plan sent back in
  the exchange with them passes.

Reply (`src/round.ts`, `pickReply` and `checkReply`):

- `progress` is cut to 200 characters before the check, like the label.
- A plan from the planner never keeps a `checkpoint` of its own. After the plan
  limit check, the server sets `checkpoint: true` when the exchange already has
  a multiple of `PLANS_PER_CHECKPOINT` plans, more than zero.
- The plan limit stays as it is, with `MAX_PLANS`.

Claude planner (`src/planners/claude.ts`, `architecture-planner.md`):

- The reply schema gets `progress`, a string; not `checkpoint`.
- The instructions name `progress` in the plan format, add the checkpoint rule
  and add the progress to the texts in the language of the request (Rules for
  the LLM).
- The last part of the prompt gives two numbers in place of "Plans left": the
  plans left before the next checkpoint and the plans left in the request.

README: `MAX_PLANS` with its new range and default, and `PLANS_PER_CHECKPOINT`
next to it.

Tests: both settings at their bounds and out of them; the 11th plan with the
default is marked and the 10th and 12th are not; a planner's own `checkpoint` is
dropped; a long `progress` is cut; a plan with `checkpoint` and `progress` in
the exchange passes the round check; the plan limit still stops at `MAX_PLANS`;
the prompt holds both numbers.
