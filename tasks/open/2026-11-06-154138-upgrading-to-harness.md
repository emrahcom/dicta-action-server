# Upgrading guide in the harness

The guide to upgrading a site's dicta moved to `dicta-action-harness`
(`docs/upgrading.md` there), where site developers and their agents work.

- Remove `docs/upgrading.md`.
- `README.md`: a short "Updating" section for this server: get the new code,
  `npm install`, restart; it keeps no data, so nothing is moved. It links to the
  harness guide for updating a whole site, and the Documents link points there
  too.
