# Test gaps

Close a gap in the final-plan tests and a race in the startup tests. No spec
changes.

- `test/round.test.ts`: a final plan whose steps all succeeded, followed by an
  `added` item, goes to the planner and its reply is sent, not done
  (`architecture.md`, Requests; `contract-plan.md`, Reporting).
- `test/index.test.ts`: `exitWithError` reads stderr on the child's `close`
  event instead of `exit`, since stderr may still be open at `exit`. The warning
  test waits until stderr has the warning instead of reading it once the
  listening line arrives.
